# Film edition facts (display only)

These facts never establish release identity or alter scoring, quality ranking,
identity corrections, monitoring, import, or acquisition. A filename/edition tag
is a claim. Runtime, distributor, aspect ratio, remaster/restoration, HDR and
release group do not prove a cut. Conflicting claims remain explicit.

## Touch of Evil (1958): IMDb tt0052311 / TMDb 1480

- **1958 theatrical:** approximately 95 minutes.
- **1958 preview:** approximately 108–109 minutes, depending on the edition/source.
- **1998 reconstructed:** approximately 110–111 minutes. Producer Rick Schmidlin
  and editor Walter Murch reconstructed the film using Welles' memo and related
  materials. Editing and the opening sound/credits differ from the theatrical
  version. It was made after Welles' death in 1985, so he never personally approved
  that reconstruction. “Director's Cut” is a release claim, not proof of approval
  or enough to relabel an otherwise unspecified file as reconstructed.

Primary/authoritative editorial sources, consulted 2026-10-19:

- [Eureka's 2024 UHD edition](https://eurekavideo.co.uk/movie/touch-of-evil-4k-ultra-hd/):
  three versions, advertised at 95 / 109 / 110 minutes. This is an edition-specific
  runtime reference, not a fingerprint.
- [Eureka's earlier edition](https://eurekavideo.co.uk/movie/touch-of-evil/): explicitly
  identifies the 1958 theatrical, 1958 preview and 1998 reconstructed versions;
  multiple aspect ratios are variants of those edits, not extra cuts.
- [Library of Congress / Michael Sragow essay](https://lcweb2.loc.gov/static/programs/national-film-preservation-board/documents/touch_evil.pdf):
  Schmidlin/Murch's 1998 reconstruction and opening sound/credit changes, based
  on the memo and additional production materials.
- [BFI programme notes](https://bfidatadigipres.github.io/big%20screen%20classics/2021/05/18/touch-of-evil/):
  reconstruction based on the 1957 memo; historical discussion of the 108-minute
  preview version.
- [Library of Congress theatre exhibition](https://www.loc.gov/exhibits/federal-theatre-project/classics.html):
  Welles' lifespan (1915–1985). Non-approval of a 1998 reconstruction follows from
  those dates; marketing terminology must not imply otherwise.

## Additive entries and parsing

The curated mapping requires an already verified TMDb identity and matching IMDb
and TMDb IDs. Title/year matching only shortlists announcements for the existing
conservative resolver. It cannot select editorial facts by itself.

Parser labels include theatrical, director's cut/DC, extended, unrated, final cut,
international cut/version, preview cut/version, reconstructed and workprint.
Filename scanning starts after the movie year and excludes a trailing release
group. Dedicated bounded edition/cut arrays are also considered. Ambiguity is
shown, never ranked. More films require source-backed entries and tests.

Only curated film candidates gain new metadata lookups. Ordinary routine
rejections retain their no-lookup behavior. A verified curated movie may make one
read-only Radarr movie lookup to compare its existing edition; missing or mismatched
existing records show Unknown. There is no research-worker dependency or new
permission for identity changes.
