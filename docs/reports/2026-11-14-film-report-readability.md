# Private film report readability

The automatic receipt-bound snapshot renderer now leads with the recorded model decision, exact source link and identification problem. Model-reported blockers show the corresponding observed values; pair-specific agreements, differences and missing or inconclusive observations are displayed separately. The report distinguishes theatrical and festival years and listing, NFO and catalogue runtimes. Numeric runtime differences are descriptive, not identity or edition scores.

If the local text policy withheld rationale or uncertainty, the report says so and labels its short summary as locally generated from retained structured choices. It does not reconstruct the original explanation. Prior refusal details, full comparison rows and legacy finding-level provenance remain available in expandable technical details. HTML escaping, source URL validation and receipt, projection and local-context bindings remain in place. The renderer does not alter the model decision, its inputs or the acquisition approval state.

Tests use invented supported, unresolved, blocked and withheld reports, including escaping, exact bindings and unchanged inputs. No live authority, provider call or production operation is added.
