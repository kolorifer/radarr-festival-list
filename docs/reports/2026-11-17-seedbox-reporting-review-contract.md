# Prospective reporting-review contract v3

## Agreed scope and demonstrated gap

PR213 distinguishes reporting approval from identity or acquisition approval in
its review instructions. Its revision2 ending nevertheless says to reject if a
material problem remains, while `material_problems` is an untyped prose list.
An invented Harbor Lantern example (listing1987, catalogue1988, matching IDs and
cast, a truthful unresolved card) demonstrates the contract gap: the old local
validator accepts a rejection naming only that unresolved year difference, with
no identified defect in reporting. This establishes an interface ambiguity, not
proof of what caused any actual model decision.

The authorized work is offline and prospective. No private case, source,
credential, provider, remote service, deployment, renewal, rearm or notification
operation is included. Earlier assessments, reviews, attempts, uncertain sends
and consumed allowances remain immutable. The unrelated CODEX_TASK is preserved.

## Small versioned change

The normal worker explicitly selects request revision3 for its next unconsumed
review. Revision1/2 builders and stored review reconstruction retain their exact
semantics and bytes. Direct legacy entry points default to revision2; historical
correction remains explicitly revision2, and a revision3 failure cannot enter
that older correction route. No new correction or repeated-generation authority
is introduced.

The new request distinguishes `source_uncertainties` from `material_problems`.
A truthful unresolved report may receive reporting approval with uncertainties.
A rejection requires at least one alleged reporting defect; approval requires
none. A defect has a bounded kind, an exact assessment/card location, relevant
observation references and an explanation. Field/value hashes are generated only
from the already disclosed assessment, redacted card and typed historical
observations, including null and empty fields. The private source URL and bot
routing are not exposed by the reference maps.

The schema declares those structures and finite reference choices. Local
validation checks field/hash pairs, unknown or hidden paths, duplicate references,
array bounds, bindings and every new prose field before retaining usable output.
The final approved-plan reconstruction dispatches the stored version and reruns
its validator. Known failures use finite sanitized diagnostics. An invalid or
uncertain attempt stays consumed; no verdict is converted, no old report is
rewritten, and no model is rerun until it produces approval.

## Validation and limits

Invented tests contrast faithful unresolved reporting with an unsupported
candidate, exaggerated blocker and omitted conflict. Connected fixtures exercise
actual request framing/parsing, typed review retention, approved-plan rebuilding,
normal rejection, invalid-reference refusal and no repeated generation/send.
Legacy request hashes are independently derived from accepted PR213; old completed
review bytes remain unchanged. The existing early-assessment worker test now
expects revision3, proving dispatch through the real step path. Installed-only
checks exercise nonempty reference validation and its refusal path.

Exact focused results, existing independent-review acceptance and supported
Python3.12/3.13 full-suite CI are recorded in the PR/checkpoint before merge. Main
independently verifies the candidate and owns any deployment decision.

These checks prove the shape and binding of what a review cites. They do not
prove that cited evidence semantically supports the alleged defect or that a
model's approval/rejection is correct. An incorrect semantic judgment remains
possible. Fixtures prove executable contract behavior, not model quality. No
film-specific exception, scoring constant, source-authentication claim or
identity/acquisition approval is added.
