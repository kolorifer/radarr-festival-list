# Normalized snapshot film-scope reassessment

This separately versioned contract assesses the underlying film rather than treating unknown edition, queue or approval as film-identity blockers. Supervisor-reviewed typed observations are evidence, not instructions or a guarantee of objective truth. The output separately records field-bound title, year, people, listing runtime, NFO runtime and identifier findings and any remaining film-identity blockers. NFO durations remain uploader-reported and retain their stream kind. Reconciliations are model interpretations for human review, and a candidate remains a proposal without identity or acquisition approval.

The existing candidate, crosswalk, source-identity, conflicting-ID, privacy, freshness and one-shot controls remain. One explicitly reviewed successor can follow the exact receipt-backed completed v3 result, with prior start, completion, retained reply, receipt and credit history bound and preserved. The successor requires a freshly observed snapshot, listing and catalogue for the same case, version, listing and asserted identities. It neither reuses the earlier grant nor permits a second child. v1, v2 and v3 serialized requests remain unchanged. The v4-specific request allowance is bounded before allocation; earlier request limits are unchanged.

The implementation and tests use invented evidence and a fake credential and transport. They do not issue live authority, contact a provider, access production data or activate a service. A separate trusted supervisor must verify original-to-normalized provenance and issue a narrowly bound authority before any actual assessment.
