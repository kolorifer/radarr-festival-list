## What changed

Movie-intel now uses exactly:
`qualified = grandfathered_deployed_224 OR Evidence Score >=225`.
The threshold is inclusive. Active routes are `grandfathered_production` and
`evidence_score_225`, both when applicable. Historical convergence/corroboration/
canon routes are retained in separate audit metadata and cannot admit new films.

The frozen result is **1,909 candidates, 224 grandfathered, 163 new, 387 qualified**.
All 224 are present exactly once and retained. The 255 research comparator is not
grandfathered: 11 of its 31 extras naturally score at least 225; the other 20 remain
unqualified. No new guards, exceptions, normalization or 250 behavior was added.

All 1,909 scores, individual family contributions, audience points, eligibility
ceilings and ranks match validated PR #5. D012 weights, proportional Audience 150,
effective ceiling 1000, canon correlation and D014 anthology attribution are unchanged.
