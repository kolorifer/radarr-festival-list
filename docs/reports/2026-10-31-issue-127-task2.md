## What changed

Final candidate validation previously reused invocation-start time. The new
regression failed before the correction for expiry during either model call:
a synthetic invocation starting1793444400 with support expiring1793444401 still
accepted a supported candidate at1793444404. Current time now advances with the
maximum of current wall time and the previous effective time plus monotonic
elapsed time since the last sample. A forward jump rebases that effective clock,
which continues advancing after rollback. Forward jumps expire support; fixed or
backward wall clocks cannot prolong it. The original event, input, evidence,
owner-decision and result identities remain unchanged/immutable.

Reconciliation runs at admission, reservations, lookups and final acceptance.
Changed decision applicability blocks further calls/acceptance, retains the
updated history and never refunds the invocation. Retained lookups mark current
freshness and still return historical records. The history's existing selected-
case/version guard remains authoritative at model reservation and supported-
result append. New in-lock guards recheck time/decision/quota before reservation
and time/decision/evidence before supported-result append, covering expiry
between external validation and persistence. An effective `checked_at` is saved
separately from original event/journal timestamps. Two-call/five-lookup, input/
output and quota semantics are preserved. Total monotonic deadline starts before
history reads and includes preflight; each model call includes its own setup
and boundary inspection in its25-second allowance.

Metadata permits one version and one help process, each at most5 seconds within
one10-second preflight deadline; no retry is introduced. The overall investigation
remains60 seconds. Nonblocking pipe readiness races are handled without restarting
a process; a wait timeout becomes an explicit refusal. Output limits and process-
group termination remain, including descendants retaining inherited pipes. The
synthetic executable uses an absolute interpreter with `-I`; successful output-
limit tests have startup headroom distinct from the deliberate short timeout test.

Client controls now cover the additional plugin, remote-plugin, browser/computer,
image-generation, code-mode host, skill-search and request-permissions families,
plus root skill-catalogue size. Exec requires its advertised `--strict-config`.

The former hand-authored safety booleans cannot approve a client: a future entry

the entire exact invocation. The check binds actual argv, executable, input/schema,
cwd and filtered environment; metadata and every call reacquire it. Failed
preflight clears stale success; model/control mutation also refuses execution.

failures were not reproduced in the fresh unmodified concurrent baselines. Startup
latency and the original narrow deadline are a plausible exposure, not a confirmed

ambient Python startup from synthetic execution. There is no universal startup
latency guarantee under arbitrary scheduler load; exceeding finite bounds still
blocks safely. Synchronous kernel process creation, filesystem operations and
process reaping are not hard real-time guarantees, even with logical deadlines.
